ISODocks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

The short version. ISODocks keeps all of your working data in locations you choose — in your Revit project, next to your model, or in a folder you nominate — and does not transmit it anywhere. The only information the add-in sends is a license verification request to the Autodesk App Store, described below. The only personal data we hold is what you send us directly by email, and what Autodesk provides to us about purchases and downloads of the app.

Who we are

ISODocks is developed and published by Spiros Manetakis, Germany. For any question about this policy or about your data, contact info.dev4revit@gmail.com.

Data handled by the add-in

ISODocks creates and reads the following, all of it on your own computer or on storage you already use for the project:

  • Settings stored inside the Revit project file itself.
  • A component mapping file saved beside your model. On a workshared project this sits next to the central model, so that your team shares one file. Where the model has no writable folder, the mapping is kept in your own user profile folder instead.
  • Title block values saved in the same place as the mapping file.
  • Starting-point defaults kept in your user profile folder.
  • The isometric drawings and PCF files that ISODocks produces, written to the output folder you choose.

None of this is uploaded, backed up, analysed, or transmitted to us or to anyone else. Where a file is written to a network location, it is because you pointed ISODocks at that location.

ISODocks contains no analytics, no telemetry, and no usage tracking.

ISODocks reads your AutoCAD Plant 3D project folder, if you configure one, in order to match the symbols and title block of the iso style you render with. It reads those files only; it does not modify them and does not send their contents anywhere.

Your project data, drawings, and models are never accessed for any purpose other than producing the documentation you request within the application.

License verification

When the add-in starts, it verifies that your Autodesk account is licensed to use it. To do this it sends your Autodesk user identifier and the app's identifier to the Autodesk App Store license service at apps.autodesk.com, which replies whether the license is valid. This identifier is an internal Autodesk reference, not your email address or name.

This request goes to Autodesk, not to us. We do not receive it, and no other information is included — no project data, no model contents, no file names, no usage details. The request is made once per session at start-up.

The result is stored on your computer so that the add-in continues to work when you are offline or the service is unavailable. If verification has not succeeded for 14 days, the add-in will ask you to reconnect.

Autodesk processes this request under its own privacy policy.

Data you send us

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include in your message. We use this solely to answer your enquiry and to provide support. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not share it with anyone.

The lawful basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and supporting our customers (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and where a purchase is involved, the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Support correspondence is retained for up to 12 months after the matter is resolved, after which it is deleted.

Data we receive from the Autodesk App Store

When you purchase or download ISODocks through the Autodesk App Store, Autodesk provides us with information about that transaction, which may include your name and email address. We use this only to fulfil the purchase, to provide support, and to meet our record-keeping obligations. We do not use it for marketing and we do not share it with third parties.

Records connected with purchases are retained for as long as required by applicable German commercial and tax law.

Please note that the Autodesk App Store, and any payment provider used to complete your purchase, process your data under their own privacy policies, which are separate from this one.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info.dev4revit@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.
