Privacy Policy — P&ID Link

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Who we are

P&ID Link is developed and published by Spiros Manetakis, Germany. For any question about this policy or about your data, contact info.dev4revit@gmail.com.

The short version

P&ID Link does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. The add-in works entirely on your own computer and makes no network connections. The only personal data we ever hold is what you send us directly by email, and what Autodesk provides to us about purchases and downloads of the app.

Data handled by the add-in

All data created by P&ID Link — the links between your Plant 3D and Revit components, your parameter mappings, and exported attribute values — is stored locally on your computer, in your own user profile folder. This data never leaves your machine. It is not uploaded, backed up, analysed, or transmitted to us or to anyone else.

P&ID Link contains no analytics, no telemetry, no usage tracking, and no update or licence checks that contact any server. It does not require an internet connection to function.

Your project data, drawings, and models are never accessed for any purpose other than performing the linking and synchronisation you request within the applications.

Data you send us

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include in your message. We use this solely to answer your enquiry and to provide support. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not share it with anyone.

The lawful basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and supporting our customers (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and where a purchase is involved, the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Support correspondence is retained for up to 12 months after the matter is resolved, after which it is deleted.

Data we receive from the Autodesk App Store

When you purchase or download P&ID Link through the Autodesk App Store, Autodesk provides us with information about that transaction, which may include your name and email address. We use this only to fulfil the purchase, to provide support, and to meet our record-keeping obligations. We do not use it for marketing and we do not share it with third parties.

Records connected with purchases are retained for as long as required by applicable German commercial and tax law.

Please note that the Autodesk App Store, and any payment provider used to complete your purchase, process your data under their own privacy policies, which are separate from this one.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info.dev4revit@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address, with a revised date at the top.
