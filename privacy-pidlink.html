P&ID Link — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This policy explains what the P&ID Link add-in for Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD Plant 3D does with data. It is published by dev4revit (Spiros Manetakis, Essen, Germany), the publisher of the app on the Autodesk App Store.

In short P&ID Link does not collect, transmit or store your drawings, models or personal documents. The only information it sends over the internet is your Autodesk user identifier and the app's own identifier, for the sole purpose of verifying your license.

1. Information sent over the internet

P&ID Link makes one kind of network request: a license check against the Autodesk licensing service, at the address apps.autodesk.com/webservices/checkentitlement.

SentPurpose
Your Autodesk user identifier To confirm that the signed-in account is entitled to use the app
The application identifier of P&ID Link To identify which app is being checked

The service replies only with whether the license is valid. The request is made when the add-in starts, and only then.

What is never sent

  • Drawing files, Revit models or their contents
  • File names, folder names or project names
  • Element data, attribute values, parameter values or the links you create
  • Your name, email address, company or IP-based location, beyond what is inherent in making an ordinary HTTPS request to Autodesk

The publisher does not receive, see or store any of this information. The request goes to Autodesk, not to dev4revit. Autodesk's own handling of it is governed by the Autodesk Privacy Statement.

Working offline

After a successful check, P&ID Link records the date of that check on your computer so it can continue working without an internet connection for 14 days. Only the date and the user identifier of the last successful check are stored, and only locally.

2. Information stored on your computer or network

P&ID Link stores the links you create, your parameter mappings and the P&ID values you export. This information stays within your own environment. It is never transmitted to the publisher or to any third party.

Single-user Revit models

For a Revit model that is not workshared, this information is stored in your Windows user profile:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\PIDLink

Workshared Revit models

When the Revit model you open is workshared, links, parameter mappings and exported P&ID values are instead stored in a folder next to the central model, so that everyone working on the project shares the same links:

<central model folder>\PIDLink_Data

This folder is on your own network or file storage, chosen by where your central model already lives. Neither the publisher nor Autodesk has any access to it. Anyone with file access to your central model folder can read these files, in the same way that they can already read the model itself.

Your own current selection in each application remains stored locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PIDLink and is not shared with colleagues.

Nothing is written into your files

P&ID Link does not embed any of its own data inside your drawings or your Revit model. The only changes it makes to a model are the parameter values you explicitly ask it to write with Sync Params, and the clearing of those values when you unlink.

3. Removing the data

Uninstalling P&ID Link does not delete the stored links and mappings. To remove them, delete the folders named above. Deleting them removes your links permanently; the drawings and the Revit model are not affected.

4. Analytics, tracking and advertising

P&ID Link contains no analytics, no usage tracking, no telemetry, no advertising and no third-party components that collect data. Nothing about how you use the add-in is reported to the publisher.

5. Your rights

Because the publisher does not collect or hold any personal data about users of P&ID Link, there is no personal data held by dev4revit for you to access, correct or erase. If you contact support by email, that correspondence is kept only for as long as needed to answer your question.

Purchases are handled by the Autodesk App Store. Any personal or payment information you provide there is held by Autodesk under their own privacy statement; the publisher receives only the information Autodesk provides to publishers about sales.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes affecting what is sent over the internet will also be described in the release notes of the version that introduces them.
